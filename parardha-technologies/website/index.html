How we help

A few of the questions we can work through together:

Where does AI actually fit?

We pinpoint where AI can make a real difference in your business, and suggest AI-native approaches that cut time and cost.

Build, buy, or partner?

We help you choose between an off-the-shelf tool, a custom build, or a third-party partner. For custom builds, we help you envision and get it built.

What will it cost, and is it worth it?

We give you a clear, honest picture of the costs involved — and what you can realistically expect to get back.

Choosing the right vendor

When you need an external vendor, we work alongside you to identify the one who will deliver — at the right cost.

What should we do first?

We help you put the work in a sensible order, starting with what's low-risk and genuinely useful.

What could go wrong?

We flag the data, privacy, and compliance issues early — before they turn into expensive problems.

Ways to work together

However you'd like to start, we keep it simple. Most people begin with a conversation.

By the hour

Pay for just what you need — a focused conversation, a second opinion, or an answer to a specific question, with no commitment beyond that.

Best if you're exploring AI for the first time, or need guidance on one particular decision.

On retainer

A simple monthly arrangement that gives you ongoing access to advice as your plans take shape over time.

Best if you're actively working on AI and want someone steady to call on.

By the project

A clearly scoped piece of work with a defined outcome — a strategy, a vendor shortlist, a roadmap, or a working prototype.

Best if you have a specific challenge that needs a clear, documented result.
