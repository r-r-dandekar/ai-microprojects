## What Parardha Technologies Does for Customers

Help SMB owners understand:

1. **Where AI helps** — Identify which business processes could benefit from AI
2. **The best approach** — Build, buy, or partner decisions
3. **Feasibility & ROI** — What's needed, what it costs, realistic returns
4. **Navigate vendor landscape** — Find actual solutions vs. hype
5. **Strategic roadmapping** — Sequence AI initiatives by priority and risk
6. **Risk assessment** — Flag compliance, data, integration issues

**Key Differentiator:** 30 years of technical/infrastructure background means you cut through hype and understand what's actually possible.
