# Use Case: Social Media Content (Marketing)

## Activity Description
Repurposing project wins, technical blog posts, and agency news into content for LinkedIn, Twitter, and other platforms.

### Problems & Inefficiencies
- **Low Consistency:** Posting only when "there's time," leading to a dead social presence.
- **Context Switching:** It's hard for a developer to switch from "coding mode" to "marketing mode" to write a tweet.
- **Repurposing Overhead:** Manually rewriting a long blog post into 5 different social formats is tedious.

## AI-Native Reimagining
Social media becomes a "Continuous Stream." AI automatically repurposes every technical achievement into a week's worth of multi-platform content. The team's only job is to "Like and Share."

### Quality & Efficiency Improvements
- **Quality:** Consistent brand presence; content is optimized for each specific platform's algorithm.
- **Efficiency:** 90% reduction in social media management time.

## AI Tool Selection
- **Primary Recommendation:** **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** - Great at capturing a specific brand voice and condensing long ideas into punchy social posts.
- **Alternative(s):** **Canva Magic Studio** - For generating accompanying visuals or social cards based on the text content.

### Specific Tool Notes
- Use "Few-shot prompting" (Principle #3) by providing 3 examples of Alphonso's best-performing social posts.

## AI-Integrated Workflow (Operational Steps)
1. **[Human Step]:** Paste a link to a new technical blog post or a summary of a project launch.
2. **[AI Step]:** Prompt: "Generate 5 LinkedIn posts and 10 Tweets based on this content. Vary the hook for each (e.g., educational, controversial, celebratory)."
3. **[AI Step]:** Ask the AI to "Suggest 3 visual concepts (images or charts) to accompany these posts."
4. **[Human Step]:** Schedule the content using a tool like Buffer or Hootsuite.
