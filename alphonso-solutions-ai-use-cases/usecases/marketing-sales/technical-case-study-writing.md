# Use Case: Technical Case Study Writing (Marketing)

## Activity Description
Creating in-depth case studies that showcase Alphonso's successful projects, highlighting technical challenges solved and business impact delivered.

### Problems & Inefficiencies
- **The "Blank Page" Problem:** Engineers are too busy to write, and marketing people don't always understand the technical depth.
- **Stale Portfolio:** Great projects are finished, but the case studies aren't written for months, missing out on sales opportunities.
- **Lack of Detail:** Case studies can end up sounding generic without specific technical details.

## AI-Native Reimagining
The case study is generated as the "Final Deliverable" of a project. AI ingests the PRD, the Git logs, and the final status report to draft a deep, technical narrative. We move from "writing a case study" to "approving a technical narrative."

### Quality & Efficiency Improvements
- **Quality:** Higher technical accuracy; better storytelling by linking code changes to business results.
- **Efficiency:** 80% reduction in time to publish new portfolio pieces.

## AI Tool Selection
- **Primary Recommendation:** **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** - Its superior long-form writing and ability to understand complex technical context make it perfect for case studies.
- **Alternative(s):** **Gemini 1.5 Pro** - Excellent for analyzing a project's entire history (PRD, Jira, Slack) to find the "pivotal moments" for the story.

### Specific Tool Notes
- Use the "Project Memory" feature in AI tools to keep track of Alphonso's specific "Success Criteria" for all case studies.

## AI-Integrated Workflow (Operational Steps)
1. **[Human Step]:** Provide the AI with the project's PRD and the "Win of the Week" summaries from the status reports.
2. **[AI Step]:** Prompt: "Draft a 1000-word technical case study. Structure it as: The Challenge, The Architecture, The Implementation, and The Impact."
3. **[AI Step]:** Ask the AI to "Highlight 3 specific technical innovations we used in this project (e.g., custom hook for state management)."
4. **[Human Step]:** Review the draft for accuracy, add specific client testimonials, and publish.
