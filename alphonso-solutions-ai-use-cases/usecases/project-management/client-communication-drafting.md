# Use Case: Client Communication Drafting (PM)

## Activity Description
Drafting emails, Slack messages, and documentation updates for clients, especially around sensitive topics like scope changes or delay notifications.

### Problems & Inefficiencies
- **"Drafting Block":** Spending 30 minutes staring at a blank screen to write a difficult 5-sentence email.
- **Miscommunication:** Poorly phrased technical explanations can confuse or frustrate non-technical clients.
- **Tone Issues:** Messages can sometimes sound too defensive or too casual in a fast-paced agency environment.

## AI-Native Reimagining
Every client communication is drafted by a "Communication Liaison" AI. The human provides the *facts* (what happened) and the *desired outcome* (what we want the client to do), and the AI provides the *perfect phrasing*.

### Quality & Efficiency Improvements
- **Quality:** Clearer, more professional communication; reduces emotional friction in difficult conversations.
- **Efficiency:** 70% reduction in time spent on routine and complex client emails.

## AI Tool Selection
- **Primary Recommendation:** **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** - Known for its high emotional intelligence and nuanced writing style.
- **Alternative(s):** **Gemini 1.5 Pro** - Great for summarizing long client email threads and drafting a "Next Steps" summary.

### Specific Tool Notes
- Create "Client Profiles" (e.g., "Client A prefers bullet points", "Client B likes technical depth") to help the AI tailor the draft.

## AI-Integrated Workflow (Operational Steps)
1. **[Human Step]:** Provide the AI with the core facts (e.g., "Feature X is delayed 2 days due to a third-party API bug").
2. **[AI Step]:** Prompt: "Draft a professional Slack update for the client explaining this delay. Focus on the solution and the impact on the overall timeline."
3. **[AI Step]:** Ask the AI to "Rewrite this to be more empathetic" or "Make it more concise for a busy CEO."
4. **[Human Step]:** Quick proofread and send.
