# Intent

## The Goal

Alphonso Solutions is a software company that does web development, mobile app development, etc. They are looking to use AI to streamline their work. Our goal is to identify places they can use AI.


## Success Looks Like
A systematic collection of specific business activities where AI can be used to help Alphonso Solutions. Each business activity should be documented in a separate .md file under /usecases, and should contain the following details:
 - Detailed description of the activity and its problems/inefficiencies
 - How this can be improved, re-imagined with AI
 - What quality and efficiency improvements will it bring?
 - List of AI tools (like Claude, Gemini, image-generation models, Canva, OCR, TTS, voice recognition, etc.)
 - Selecting more appropriate tools
 - Specific notes about usage or features of those AI tools related to the task
 - AI-integrated workflow for the activity

## Details about Alphonso
Alphonso Solutions is a boutique software engineering agency (10–50 people).

    Key Characteristics: Highly agile, flat team structure, and focused on building custom web and mobile applications for startups and small-to-midsize businesses (SMBs).

    The Strategic Reality: Because you are a small team, you don't have the luxury of massive, specialized departments. Team members often wear multiple hats (e.g., developers doing their own QA or project management), meaning manual overhead directly drains engineering capacity.

### Business Model
Alphonso operates on a Fixed-Price Business Model for custom software delivery.

    The Financial Mechanics: You estimate project scope upfront and charge a set total fee.

    The AI Advantage: In an hourly billing model, efficiency cuts your revenue. But in your fixed-price model, efficiency equals pure profit. If AI allows a developer to complete a $20,000 project in 40 hours instead of 80, your delivery costs drop by 50% while your top-line revenue remains identical.

### Tasks
The core tasks consuming Alphonso's day-to-day operations include:

    Pre-Dev: Client discovery, gathering messy requirements, and drafting formal scoping documents/PRDs.

    Core Coding: Writing boilerplate code, setting up databases, configuring APIs, and front-end styling.

    QA & Debugging: Manual cross-browser/cross-device testing, hunting down edge-case bugs, and fixing regressions.

    Project Management: Standardizing client communication, tracking milestones, and handling internal status updates.

AI could be used to help them in areas like:
 - Software engineering and coding
 - Quality assurance and testing
 - Marketing and publicity
 - Product management
 - Business analytics
 - Operations and resources allocation
 - Pre-Dev & Scoping
 - Project Management
