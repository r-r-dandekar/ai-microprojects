# Decisions

This file records the decisions made during the brainstorming and planning phase for identifying AI use cases for Alphonso Solutions.

## Coverage & Prioritization
- **Decision:** We will cover all important business activities rather than focusing heavily on just one. The goal is to provide a comprehensive systematic collection of AI use cases across all core functions of the agency.

## Primary Business Areas
- **Decision:** We will focus on the **Full-Spectrum Agency** lifecycle.
- **Included Areas:** Pre-Dev & Scoping, Core Coding, QA & Debugging, Project Management, and Marketing/Sales.

## Workflow Detail Level
- **Decision:** Use **Operational steps**.
- **Description:** A step-by-step procedure detailing human actions vs. AI tool actions to ensure clear, actionable guidance for implementation.

## AI Tool Selection Strategy
- **Decision:** Use **Curated Options**.
- **Description:** Provide a primary recommendation with 1-2 alternatives based on specific strengths to give clear starting points while acknowledging tool specialization.

## Sample Task Definition
- **Decision:** Use **Granular Tasks**.
- **Description:** Identify 3-5 specific, recurring sub-tasks for each area to make AI intervention tangible and easy to adopt.

## Quantifying Improvements
- **Decision:** Use a **Hybrid approach**.
- **Description:** Combine qualitative descriptions for quality improvements with estimated time-savings percentages for efficiency gains.

## File Organization
- **Decision:** Use **Category-based folders**.
- **Description:** Organize use cases into subfolders within `/usecases` based on the 5 primary business areas (e.g., `/usecases/core-coding/`).

## Standardization & Templates
- **Decision:** Provide **Both Template and Principles**.
- **Description:** Create a `usecase-template.md` for structural consistency and a `global-ai-principles.md` for high-quality prompting guidance across all tasks.

## Vision Integration
- **Decision:** Use **Both Standalone and Integrated**.
- **Description:** Maintain a standalone vision document (`004-vision.md`) and include specific "Reimagining" notes in each use case file to bridge philosophy and practice.

## Pre-Dev & Scoping: Granular Tasks
- **Decision:** Document **Discovery Note Cleanup, PRD Generation, and Feature Estimation**.
- **Reasoning:** These are labor-intensive and have high "profit-multiplier" potential for a fixed-price agency.

## Core Coding & QA: Granular Tasks
- **Decision:** Document **Boilerplate Setup, API Integration, UI Styling** (Coding) and **Unit Test Generation, Bug Reproduction, Regression Testing** (QA).
- **Reasoning:** Targets the "Strategic Reality" where developers handle their own QA and manual overhead drains capacity.

## PM & Marketing: Granular Tasks
- **Decision:** Document **Status Report Automation, Client Communication Drafting, Milestone Tracking** (PM) and **Technical Case Study Writing, Social Media Content, Sales Deck Personalization** (Marketing).
- **Reasoning:** Leverages LLM strengths in drafting and synthesis to reduce non-engineering capacity drain.
